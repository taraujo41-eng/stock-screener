A+ Swing Trade Criteria & Strategy
[1] Universe & Liquidity Filter (Base Scan)
  • Market Capitalization: ≥ $10 Billion (Mega / Large-Cap only)
  • Average Daily Volume: ≥ 2,000,000 shares (20-day SMA)
  • Share Price: ≥ $20.00
  • Option Chain Liquidity: Tight bid-ask spread (≤ $0.05 to $0.10 near ATM), Open Interest ≥ 500 across target strikes
[2] Multi-Timeframe Trend & Momentum (Daily Chart)
  • Primary Trend: Daily Close > 50-day SMA (Longs) / Daily Close < 50-day SMA (Shorts), plus extreme reversals at 3SD BB or 52-Week High/Low
  • Short-Term Momentum & 52-Week Levels:
    • 8 EMA Trend Pullback: Price touches or closes near 8 EMA while 8 EMA is sloped in trend direction.
    • Key Level Breakout: Daily close breaks out from consolidation with 8 EMA > 21 EMA.
    • 3SD Bollinger Band Reversal: Daily price touching 3-Standard-Deviation Bollinger Band with confirmed RSI divergence.
    • 52-Week High / Low Setup: Stock is Hits (0%), At (≤ 3%), or Near (≤ 5%) its 52-Week High or 52-Week Low (trend & candle-aware: bounces/breakouts = Bullish, rejections/breakdowns = Bearish).
  • Trend Strength: ADX(14) ≥ 20 and rising (confirms non-choppy regime)
  • Overextension / Reversal: Daily RSI(14) between 45–60 on trend pullbacks; OR touching 3SD band / 52-Week High or Low with divergence or reversal candle
[3] Volume & Catalyst Confluence
  • Relative Volume (RVOL): ≥ 1.2x to 1.5x on setup day / breakout candle
  • 52-Week High / Low Confluence: Score boost & badge when price is Hits (+3), At ≤ 3% (+2), or Near ≤ 5% (+1) of 52-Week High or 52-Week Low
  • Earnings / Binary Event: Safe: NO earnings or major corporate catalysts within next 14 calendar days (avoids IV crush & gap risk)
[4] Execution & Sizing Plan
  • Expiration (DTE): 30 to 60 DTE (prevents rapid front-month theta decay)
  • Target Delta: 0.45 to 0.60 (Directional ATM / Slight ITM)
  • Risk-to-Reward Ratio: Minimum 2:1 required (Target 1: 2.0R Trim 50%, Target 2: 3.2R Runner)
  • Institutional Pre-Trade Log: Tap "Copy Pre-Trade Log (Template)" on any card for an instant copy-paste trading journal entry.
